Taking action

How to get help — step by step

Taking the first step is often the hardest — especially when you're juggling classes, family expectations, and financial pressure. Here's how to begin.

1

Acknowledge your feelings

Notice and name what you're experiencing without judgment. Recognizing that something is wrong is a courageous and necessary first step.

2

Talk to someone you trust

Share what you're going through with a friend, family member, or colleague. You don't need to have all the answers — just opening up helps.

3

Visit your campus health or wellness center

Your campus counselor, wellness office, or student health clinic can be a good place to start. They can connect you with mental health support and resources.

4

Find a mental health professional

A psychologist, therapist, or psychiatrist can provide diagnosis and treatment. Many now offer online appointments for accessibility.

5

Give yourself permission to not be okay right now

Healing isn't linear. There will be setbacks. Celebrate small wins, adjust your approach when needed, and keep going.
