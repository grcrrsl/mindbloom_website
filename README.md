🌿 Mind Bloom

Mind Bloom is a student-focused mental health education website designed to help college students and young adults understand mental health, reduce stigma, and access practical resources that support their well-being.

📌 Project Overview

Mental health challenges such as stress, anxiety, burnout, and isolation are common among students and young adults. However, many individuals lack access to clear, reliable, and easy-to-understand mental health information.

Mind Bloom was created to provide accessible mental health education, coping strategies, self-care tools, and support resources in a welcoming and user-friendly platform.

🎯 Objectives

To promote mental health awareness among college students and young adults.
To provide clear, reliable, and easy-to-understand educational resources about mental health and self-care. 
To help users recognize signs of stress, anxiety, and other mental health challenges. 
To encourage positive coping strategies, self-improvement, and emotional well-being.
To create an accessible and user-friendly online platform that supports mental wellness education.
